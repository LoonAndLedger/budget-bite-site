Budget Bite

A week of high-protein meals, planned around your budget.

Tell Budget Bite your protein goal and your weekly grocery budget. It builds a full week of meals and a grocery list that stays inside what you can spend.

iPhone app — coming soon to the App Store

What it does

Plans around your goals

Set a daily protein target and a weekly budget, and get a seven-day plan with recipes.

A grocery list that makes sense

One consolidated shopping list, sorted by store section, with photos so you can spot items.

Fits how you eat

Dietary preferences, allergies, and fasting days, so meat-free days are planned for you.

Track what you actually eat

Check off meals, add anything extra, and see calories and protein for the day.

About

Budget Bite is built for students and anyone who wants to hit a protein goal without overspending on groceries. It is currently in development and has not been released yet.
